✏️ Whiteboard Pen

Write on any website while you keep scrolling and clicking it. No add-on, no Developer mode.

Step 1 - Put the button on your bookmarks bar (one time)

Press Ctrl + Shift + B so the bookmarks bar is visible. Then drag this blue button up onto the bookmarks bar and let go:

✏ Pen

Drag it with the mouse. Do not just click it on this page.

Step 2 - Use it on any website

  1. Open any website (Google, Wikipedia, NCERT, Gemini...).
  2. Click ✏ Pen on your bookmarks bar. A toolbar appears at the bottom.
  3. Arrow = use the page normally (click, type, scroll). Pen / highlighter / shapes / eraser = mark. The mouse wheel still scrolls while you mark.
  4. Marks stay in place on the page when you scroll.
  5. Click ✏ Pen again to hide or show the toolbar.

If dragging does not work

  1. Right-click the bookmarks bar and choose Add page.
  2. Name it Pen. Open the file pen_bookmarklet.txt, copy everything in it, and paste it into the URL box. Save.

Good to know

  • You must click it again after you open a new page or reload.
  • It does not work on chrome:// pages or inside Chrome's built-in PDF viewer.
